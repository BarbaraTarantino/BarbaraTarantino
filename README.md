# Barbara Tarantino, Ph.D.

Postdoctoral Researcher – Causal AI and Biomedical Machine Learning  
University of Pavia – Department of Brain and Behavioural Sciences

---

## Profile

I am a researcher working at the intersection of **causal inference**, **machine learning**, and **computational biology**.

My research focuses on developing methodological frameworks that combine **structural causal modeling** with **deep learning** to improve the interpretability, robustness, and scientific validity of AI models applied to biomedical and genomic data.

In particular, I develop methods for:

- **causal structure learning**
- **hybrid deep–causal modeling**
- **structural evaluation of machine learning models**
- **mechanistic interpretation of AI systems in biology**

## Research Projects

### Structural Causal Modeling

**SEMgraph**  
Structural Equation Modeling framework for causal network reconstruction and validation in biological systems.  
CRAN: https://cran.r-project.org/package=SEMgraph

**SEMdeep**  
Hybrid framework integrating deep neural networks with Structural Equation Models for multimodal biomarker discovery.  
CRAN: https://cran.r-project.org/package=SEMdeep

### Structural Evaluation of AI Models

**ISAAC (in development)**  
Intervention-based framework for evaluating machine learning models through controlled structural perturbations.

Current application:  
- transcription factor binding prediction from genomic sequences

Repository:  
https://github.com/BarbaraTarantino/isaac-xai-tf

### Teaching Resources

**AI for Genomic Data Science**

Graduate-level course covering machine learning and deep learning methods for biomedical and genomic data, including reproducible tutorials and notebooks.

Repository:  
https://github.com/BarbaraTarantino/AI-for-Genomic-Data-Science
